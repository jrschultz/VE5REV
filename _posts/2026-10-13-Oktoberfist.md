## Oktoberfist!

I had the notion for a fun fall morse code challenge that I call _**Oktoberfist!**_ 

+ 10, 10 contact CW Activations (POTA) in October
+ 10 watts or less
+ 10MHz (30m Band)
+ 10WPM or more  

Give it a whirl and get on the air!

73 de VE5REV
